FLORA

Field Leaf Observer & Root Assistant

The fleet's gardener. FLORA feels the soil, the air and the light around a plant, and waters it when it needs it, unless AURA, the weather station, says rain is on the way.

Overview

Soil, air, light, water

Soil

How thirsty

A soil fork, calibrated from her page, read once a minute and powered only while it reads, so it doesn't corrode away.

Air

Temperature and humidity

An AHT10, the same as NORA and MILA. AURA plots it with the rest of the fleet, in FLORA's green.

Light

Sun and UV

A photoresistor, like MILA's: brightness and the hours of good light today. A UV module, like KIDA-01's: the UV index.

Water

A pump, carefully

She waters when the soil is dry: never longer than 10 seconds, never more than 4 times a day, not in the midday heat.

Weather

Asks AURA first

If AURA, the weather station, thinks rain is coming, she lets the sky do it.

Fleet

One of the robots

Joins the fleet's network, beacons over ESP-NOW and Bluetooth LE, talks in Brainfuck on her buzzer, and RIFT can ask her to water.

Watering

Only when it's safe and needed

Automatic watering starts off: calibrate the fork, test the pump, then turn it on. She waters only when the soil is drier than your setting, and never:

  • for more than 10 seconds (and a hard 15-second cut-off whatever happens),
  • within 30 minutes of the last watering, or more than 4 times a day,
  • in the midday heat, unless the soil is critically dry,
  • when AURA thinks rain is coming, or rain is forecast within 3 hours,
  • when the fork reads nonsense.

The rules are plain C++, tested on the PC (tests/test_rules.cpp), and every number is a setting on her page.
